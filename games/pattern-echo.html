Pattern Echo

Watch the pattern, then repeat it. Each round adds one more step.

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Press Start and watch the pads light up.
  2. When it says Your turn, repeat the pattern by clicking, tapping, or pressing keys 1 to 4.
  3. Get it right and the pattern grows by one step.
  4. One wrong pad ends the game. How many rounds can you complete?

Good to know

  • Each pad plays its own musical note. Switch sound on or off with the Sound button or the M key.
  • Each pad has its own shape and number, so you never have to rely on colour alone.
  • Your best round is saved in this browser only.

About Pattern Echo

Pattern Echo trains short-term memory. The pattern gets longer by one step every round, so you will have to find your own way to remember it, such as chunking it into groups of three or saying the numbers aloud.

It is an original sequence-memory game made for PlayNova.
