The short version

PlayNova does not ask for your name, email or any account. The site does not collect personal information. Your game scores and settings stay in your own browser.

What PlayNova saves on your device

To make the games work, PlayNova stores a few small items in your browser's local storage:

  • Your favorite games.
  • Your recently played games and how often you opened each one.
  • Your best score in each game.
  • Your sound on/off choice.

This information never leaves your device through PlayNova. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

What PlayNova does not do

  • It does not ask for or store personal details, payment details or precise location.
  • It does not use accounts, logins or tracking cookies.
  • It does not currently use analytics or advertising services.

Hosting

PlayNova is delivered by Cloudflare, a web hosting company. Like almost every website, the host may automatically record technical information such as your IP address and the pages requested, for security and to run the service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising in the future

PlayNova may show ads in the future. If that happens, ad companies may use cookies or similar technology, and this policy will be updated before ads appear, including any consent choices that are required.

Children

PlayNova's games are suitable for all ages. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
