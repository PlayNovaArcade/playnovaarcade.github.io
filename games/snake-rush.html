Snake Rush

Eat the orbs, grow longer, and do not crash. It speeds up with every bite.

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Press Start, an arrow key, or swipe to begin.
  2. Steer with the arrow keys or W A S D. On a phone, swipe on the board or use the buttons.
  3. Each pink orb is 1 point and makes you longer and faster. Golden stars are worth 5, but they disappear quickly.
  4. In Classic walls mode, hitting the edge ends the game. In No walls mode you come out the other side. Hitting yourself always ends it.

Good to know

  • Press Space or P to pause. The game also pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Your best score for each mode is saved in this browser only.
  • Sound effects can be switched off with the Sound button or the M key.

About Snake Rush

Snake Rush is PlayNova's take on the classic snake game. Every orb you eat makes the snake a little longer and a little faster, so the board fills up and every turn matters more.

Tip: stay close to the edges early on and leave the middle open. Plan a route back out before you dive in for a golden star.
