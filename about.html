About PlayNova

Play More. Discover More.

PlayNova is a free website of simple browser games. Open a game and play right away: there is nothing to download, no account to create and nothing to pay.

What you will find here

  • Original mini-games that are quick to learn and hard to put down.
  • Arcade, reaction, memory, puzzle and strategy games for all ages, with sound effects you can switch off.
  • Favorites, recent games and best scores that are saved on your own device.

How PlayNova treats your data

Your scores and favorites are stored in your own browser, not on a server. PlayNova does not ask for your name or email to play. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Who makes PlayNova

PlayNova is made by Majid, who enjoys building games and wanted a simple place to play them without ads or accounts getting in the way.
