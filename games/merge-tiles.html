Merge Tiles

Slide the tiles. Matching numbers merge into one. Can you reach 2048?

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Use the arrow keys or W A S D, or swipe on the board, to slide every tile at once.
  2. Two tiles with the same number join into one tile worth double.
  3. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move.
  4. The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge. Reach 2048 to win, then keep going if you like.

Good to know

  • Every merge adds its value to your score.
  • Your best score is saved in this browser only, and it is saved as you play.
  • Tiles always show their number, so colour is only a bonus.

About Merge Tiles

Merge Tiles is a sliding number puzzle in the style of the well-known 2048 game. It is easy to learn, but the board fills up fast if you move without a plan.

A popular strategy: keep your biggest tile in one corner and build a chain of smaller tiles next to it. Try to avoid the one direction that would pull it out of its corner.
