Reaction Speed Test

Wait for the pad to turn green, then react as fast as you can.

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Press the pad to start a round.
  2. Wait while it is red. Do not click yet.
  3. The moment it turns green, click, tap, or press Space or Enter.
  4. Your time appears in milliseconds. Press the pad again for another round.

Good to know

  • Clicking too early is a false start. The pad locks for one second as a penalty.
  • Your best time is saved in this browser only.
  • Results are approximate. Your screen, device and browser add a small delay, so compare your own scores over time rather than with other people's.

About Reaction Speed Test

Reaction time is the gap between seeing a signal and responding to it. In this test the wait before the signal is random, so you cannot predict it and have to actually watch.

Many people land somewhere between 200 and 350 milliseconds in simple tests like this, but it varies with the device, the screen and how alert you are. Play a few rounds and look at your average to see a more honest picture than a single lucky click.
