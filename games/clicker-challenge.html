Clicker Challenge

Click or tap as fast as you can for 15 seconds.

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Press the big button to start. That first press counts as your first click.
  2. Keep clicking or tapping until the timer reaches zero.
  3. Your total clicks and clicks per second appear when time is up.
  4. Press Play again to try for a better score.

Good to know

  • Clicks after time runs out are ignored.
  • Holding a key down does not count as repeated clicks.
  • Your best score is saved in this browser only. Results depend on your device, mouse or screen.

About Clicker Challenge

Clicker Challenge is a short burst of speed: fifteen seconds, one button, and your own record to beat.

On a touchscreen, tapping with two fingers can help. On a computer, a steady rhythm usually beats frantic mashing.
