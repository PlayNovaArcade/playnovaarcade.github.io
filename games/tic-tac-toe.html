How to play

  1. Choose Two players or Vs computer.
  2. X goes first. Click or tap an empty square to place your mark.
  3. Get three in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win.
  4. If all nine squares fill up with no winner, the round is a draw.

Good to know

  • In computer mode you play X and the computer plays O. The computer is simple: it takes a win if it can, blocks yours, then picks a good open square. It can be beaten.
  • The scoreboard counts this session only. It resets when you change mode or leave the page.
  • You can play with the keyboard: Tab to a square and press Enter or Space.

About Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-Tac-Toe is one of the oldest paper-and-pencil games. With perfect play from both sides it always ends in a draw, so the fun is in setting traps.

Try for a fork: a move that creates two ways to win at once. Your opponent can only block one of them.
