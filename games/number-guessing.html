Number Guessing

Find the secret number using only higher and lower clues.

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty. It sets the range of the secret number.
  2. Type a whole number in the box and press Guess.
  3. You are told whether your guess is too high or too low.
  4. Keep narrowing the range until you find it. A new secret number is picked every round.

Good to know

  • Smart strategy: always guess the middle of the range that is left. That finds any number in the fewest possible guesses, shown as the smart limit above the game.
  • Out-of-range and repeated guesses do not count as attempts.
  • Your fewest guesses for each difficulty is saved in this browser only.

About Number Guessing

Number Guessing is a small logic puzzle in disguise. Each answer removes part of the range, so the best players do not guess randomly. They cut the range in half every time.

That halving idea is called binary search, and computers use it to find things in huge sorted lists. On the Easy range of 1 to 50, six guesses are always enough if you play it well.
