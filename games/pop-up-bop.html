Pop-Up Bop

Bop the critters before they hide. Leave the spiky ones alone!

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Press Start. After a 3-2-1 countdown you have 30 seconds.
  2. Click or tap a critter as soon as it pops up, or press its number key (1 to 9, top row is 1 2 3).
  3. Blue critters are worth 1 point. Golden ones with a crown are worth 5.
  4. Red spiky critters cost you 3 points. Let them hide.

Good to know

  • Hit five in a row to raise your combo multiplier, up to x4. Missing a critter or bopping an empty hole resets it.
  • Critters appear faster and hide sooner as the round goes on.
  • Your best score is saved in this browser only.

About Pop-Up Bop

Pop-Up Bop is a quick reaction game inspired by classic fairground games. It rewards both speed and care, because the spiky critters punish wild tapping.

Tip: watch the whole board instead of one hole, and keep your combo going. A x4 multiplier on a golden critter is worth 20 points.
