Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time and find every matching pair.

Back to all games

This game needs JavaScript. Please turn it on and reload the page.

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  2. Click or tap a card to flip it, then flip a second card.
  3. Matching cards stay face up. Cards that do not match flip back over.
  4. Match every pair to win. Fewer moves and a faster time make a better result.

Good to know

  • Every symbol has its own shape, so you never have to rely on colour alone.
  • With a keyboard, Tab to the board, use the arrow keys to move between cards, and press Enter or Space to flip.
  • Your best result for each difficulty is saved in this browser only.

About Memory Match

Memory Match is a classic concentration game. Every card has a partner, and the challenge is remembering where each symbol was hiding.

Easy has six pairs, Medium has eight and Hard has twelve. A good habit is to flip cards in an order you can recall, and to say the symbol and its position to yourself as you go.
